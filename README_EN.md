[h1]Civilization: Typhon Empire[/h1]
[h2]Unique ability: Empire of the Extraordinary[/h2]
Land military units trained in a city with a Campus (or Mage Tower) start with a free promotion. Campus and Holy Site districts provide +1 Great General point per turn. Cities with an Encampment ignore pressure from Religions founded by other civilizations.
[h2]Unique unit: Battle Mage[/h2]
Typhon's Medieval era ranged unit, replaces the Crossbowman and is never replaced. Its strength grows as technology advances. +5 Ranged Strength when adjacent to at least one other Battle Mage. Corps and armies heal every turn; an army can cast the weapon of mass destruction Annihilation Scar once, and the whole army dies after casting.
[h2]Unique district: Mage Tower[/h2]
Typhon's scientific district, replaces the Campus and is cheaper to build. Keeps all Campus adjacency bonuses and adds +2 Science for each adjacent Encampment. The Mage Tower is defensible with a ranged attack, and enemy Spies operate at 1 level below normal in this city.
[h2]Unique building: Burnstone Acidification Works[/h2]
Typhon's unique building, replaces the Factory. Its +6 Production and +3 Science bonus is extended to all City Centers within 6 tiles. Farms in this city provide +5 Food, and Mines and Quarries in this city provide +2 Production.
[/code]

[h1]Leader: Rosetta Augustus[/h1]
[h2]Leader's Ability: Resilience of Mortals[/h2]
Entering a new era grants 1 random Eureka and 1 random Inspiration from that era, or 2 of each if it is a Dark Age.
Killing an enemy unit in combat grants the Eureka for that unit's prerequisite technology if it has not been researched yet (once per technology).
[h2]Agenda: Kindred[/h2]
Always tries to keep a strong army. Likes civilizations whose land forces are no weaker than his. Dislikes civilizations that spread their Religion to his cities.
[h2]Recommended Playstyle[/h2]
tldr: Domination Victory first, Science Victory second
Typhon is a civilization whose Campuses and Holy Sites both work for the army. Plan Mage Towers and Encampments in pairs: a Mage Tower next to an Encampment earns a large Science bonus, land units trained in a city with a Mage Tower start with a free promotion, and your Campuses and Holy Sites keep producing Great General points. Cities with an Encampment shrug off foreign religious pressure, which keeps your belief yields safe. Battle Mages grow stronger on their own as research advances, and once formed into an army they can strike with Annihilation Scar, a weapon of mass destruction ahead of its era. The Burnstone Acidification Works makes farms and mines thrive and adds Production and Science. Rosetta himself is a catch-up leader: every new era, and a Dark Age most of all, brings extra Eurekas and Inspirations, and picking fights with a more advanced rival pays out in Eurekas too. Late in the game, Desanctification after Mass Media turns hoarded Faith into Science and Diplomatic Favor, and the Theocratic Council gives you a way to weaken someone else's religion.
[/code]

[h2]City project: Desanctification[/h2]
Typhon only. Requires a Mage Tower district and unlocks with the Mass Media civic. Repeatable, and the cost grows with game progress. On completion, half of your Faith is converted into an equal amount of Science, and you gain 25 Diplomatic Favor.
[h2]World Congress: Theocratic Council[/h2]
A new World Congress resolution that appears from the Modern era and targets one founded Religion.
Desanctification: -10 Strength (Combat and Religious) for all units of the chosen Religion.
Secular Oversight: all players may condemn units of the chosen Religion, and each condemnation grants 25 Diplomatic Favor.
It joins the resolution pool whenever the MOD is enabled, whether or not Typhon is in the game.
[/code]

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
Requires the Gathering Storm ruleset, Rise and Fall, and the Ethiopia Pack.
With the [b]Cecil Empire[/b] MOD enabled as well, Rosetta Augustus and Gawain Cecil each get a diplomatic attitude toward the other.
This MOD replaces the Unit Panel script so that only Annihilation Scar is shown, and it chains with other MODs that also change the Unit Panel following the community convention. If another UI MOD replaces the Unit Panel outright, extra base-game nuclear weapon buttons may appear on the Battle Mage.
If you run into any issues, please provide the list of MODs and the Database.log and Lua.log files.
