[b]This MOD adds the civilization Typhon Empire from the online novel "The Sword of Dawn", led by Emperor Rosetta Augustus.[/b]

[code]
[h1]Civilization: Typhon Empire[/h1]
[h2]Unique ability: Empire of the Extraordinary[/h2]
Land military units trained in a city with a Campus (or Mage Tower) start with a free promotion.
Campus and Holy Site districts provide +1 Great General point per turn.
Cities with an Encampment ignore pressure from Religions founded by other civilizations.
[h2]Unique unit: Battle Mage[/h2]
Typhon's Medieval era ranged unit, replaces the Crossbowman. Unlocked by Machinery, costs 180. It never upgrades: after Ballistics it gains +20 melee Combat Strength and +20 Ranged Strength when attacking, after Advanced Ballistics another +20 melee and +25 when attacking, and it keeps every promotion it has earned. +5 Ranged Strength when adjacent to at least one other Battle Mage (does not stack). Corps and armies heal every turn. An army can cast Annihilation Scar once; the whole army dies after casting.
[h2]Unique district: Mage Tower[/h2]
Typhon's scientific district, replaces the Campus and is cheaper to build. Keeps all Campus adjacency bonuses and adds +2 Science for each adjacent Encampment. The Mage Tower is defensible with a ranged attack, and enemy Spies operate at 1 level below normal in this city.
[h2]Unique building: Burnstone Acidification Works[/h2]
Typhon's unique building, replaces the Factory. Unlocked by Industrialization. Its +6 Production and +3 Science bonus is extended to all City Centers within 6 tiles. Farms in this city provide +5 Food, and Mines and Quarries in this city provide +2 Production; these two bonuses do not extend to other cities.
Also included: a loyalty lens overlay with the Typhon emblem.
[/code]

[h2]Annihilation Scar[/h2]
Built on the game's native weapon of mass destruction system, but presented as a skill of the Battle Mage army: only a Battle Mage in army formation shows the button, you have one cast per army, and the whole army dies after casting. Blast radius 1. It hurts units and buildings only, not population, improvements or roads, and leaves no fallout. Corps need the Nationalism civic and armies need the Mobilization civic, as in the base game.
[h2]Battlefield Regeneration[/h2]
Battle Mage corps and armies recover 10 health at the start of every turn.
[h2]City project: Desanctification[/h2]
Typhon only. Requires a Mage Tower district and unlocks with the Mass Media civic. Repeatable, and the cost grows with game progress. On completion, half of your Faith is converted into an equal amount of Science toward the current research, and you gain 25 Diplomatic Favor.
[h2]World Congress: Theocratic Council[/h2]
A new World Congress resolution that appears from the Modern era. It targets one founded Religion, with two options.
Desanctification: -10 Strength (Combat and Religious) for all units of the chosen Religion.
Secular Oversight: all players may condemn units of the chosen Religion, and each condemnation grants 25 Diplomatic Favor.
It joins the resolution pool whenever the MOD is enabled, whether or not Typhon is in the game.
[/code]

[h1]Leader: Rosetta Augustus[/h1]
[h2]Leader's Ability: Resilience of Mortals[/h2]
Entering a new era grants 1 random Eureka and 1 random Inspiration from that era, or 2 of each if it is a Dark Age.
Killing an enemy unit in combat grants the Eureka for that unit's prerequisite technology if it has not been researched yet (once per technology).
[h2]Agenda: Kindred[/h2]
Always tries to keep a strong army. Likes civilizations whose land forces are no weaker than his. Dislikes civilizations that spread their Religion to his cities.
[h2]Recommended Playstyle[/h2]
tldr: Domination Victory first, Science Victory second
Typhon is a civilization whose Campuses and Holy Sites both work for the army. Plan Mage Towers and Encampments in pairs: a Mage Tower next to an Encampment earns a large Science bonus, land units trained in a city with a Mage Tower start with a free promotion, and your Campuses and Holy Sites keep producing Great General points. Cities with an Encampment shrug off foreign religious pressure, so missionaries cannot soften your heartland. Battle Mages never need upgrading, since Ballistics and Advanced Ballistics make them stronger on their own. Form three into an army, end a fight with Annihilation Scar, and keep the rest of your force healing with Battlefield Regeneration. The Burnstone Acidification Works makes farms and mines thrive and adds Production and Science. Rosetta himself is a catch-up leader: every new era, and a Dark Age most of all, brings extra Eurekas and Inspirations, and picking fights with a more advanced rival pays out in Eurekas too. Late in the game, Desanctification after Mass Media turns hoarded Faith into Science and Diplomatic Favor, and the Theocratic Council gives you a way to weaken someone else's religion.
[/code]

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
Requires the Gathering Storm ruleset, Rise and Fall, and the Ethiopia Pack (the Mage Tower district reuses its Alchemical Society model). Please start a new game rather than enabling the MOD in an existing save. Multiplayer sync has not been specifically tested.
[b]Cecil Empire:[/b] when both MODs are enabled, Rosetta Augustus and Gawain Cecil each get a diplomatic attitude toward the other: "a rival is still a kindred spirit". This content only loads when the Cecil Empire MOD is detected, so nothing changes if you do not have it.
[b]Dream City:[/b] there is no dedicated linkage. The two MODs do not change the same content and can be enabled together.
This MOD replaces the Unit Panel script so that only Annihilation Scar is shown, and it chains with other MODs that also change the Unit Panel (for example the Klee MOD) following the community convention. If another UI MOD replaces the Unit Panel outright, the official nuclear weapon buttons may reappear on the Battle Mage.
Annihilation Scar uses the native nuclear weapon system, so the native diplomatic consequences still apply and this MOD adds no extra penalty.
If you run into any issues, please provide the list of MODs and the Database.log and Lua.log files.
